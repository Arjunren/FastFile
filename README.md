# 📖 FastFile User Guide

FastFile is a fast, temporary plain text sharing tool. Follow the simple steps below to share and download files.

---

## 📤 How to Share TXT Files

1. **Select Files**: Open FastFile on the **Upload** tab and drag & drop your `.txt` files into the box, or click the box to browse.


2. **Upload**: Click the **Save & Share Files** button.


3. **Copy Secret Key**: Once uploaded, a unique 6-character code (e.g., `A1B2C3`) will appear. Click **Copy Key**.


4. **Share**: Send the 6-character key to your recipient.



---

## 📥 How to Receive & Download Files

1. **Switch Tab**: Click the **Download** tab at the top of the page.


2. **Enter Key**: Type or paste the 6-character secret key into the search box.


3. **Search**: Click **Search** to find the shared files.


4. **View or Save**:
* Click **View** to preview the text directly in your browser.


* Click **Download** to save the `.txt` file onto your device.





---

## ⚠️ Important System Limits

* **Supported File Type**: Plain text (`.txt`) files only.


* **File Size Limit**: Maximum **900 KB** per file.


* **Expiration**: All secret keys and files automatically expire after **24 hours**.
